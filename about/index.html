About the Author

Mike Rizek

Author and framework developer — constitutional theory, geopolitical security, AI governance.

Mike Rizek is an author and framework developer working at the intersection of constitutional theory, geopolitical security, and AI governance. He is the author of Trust Architecture, seven constitutional conditions for AI infrastructure.

In more than twenty years in IT, much of it as an application systems engineer, he instrumented live systems for security and performance for the United States government and leading American corporations. That work was built on one premise he carries into constitutional design: a system that cannot be observed while it runs cannot be trusted afterward.

The papers published here apply that premise across three domains. Trust Architecture sets out the constitutional conditions for AI infrastructure. The New Great Wall and Dome applies the same method to defensive posture between sovereign powers. A further article tests it against the Middle East conflict.

KyaniteShift is an independent project. The framework papers and articles published here are his.
